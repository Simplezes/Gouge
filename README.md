## How it works

While falling, **right-click** with a pickaxe facing a wall to activate the slide. Your pickaxe digs into the surface and friction takes over, bleeding off speed as you scrape down. The harder the block, the more it slows you.

On a **hard block**, friction keeps eating away at your momentum tick after tick no button needed until you've genuinely lost all your speed and come to a stop. Once you're stopped, you lock in place and hang there until your grip gives out or your pickaxe takes too much punishment. When the timer runs out, you slip and there's a cooldown before you can hang again.

On a **soft block**, there's no locking, you just keep scraping, and it takes noticeably more damage off your landing than it used to.

**Double-tap Crouch** release and re-press within about half a second to kick off the wall, whether you're locked in place or still mid-slide.

Higher-tier pickaxes hang longer and drain durability slower. Sliding also deals damage to any players you barrel into at high speed.

## Enchantments

| Enchantment | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Grip** (I-III) | Extends how long you can hang before slipping |
| **Momentum** (I-III) | Reduces the cooldown after slipping |

Both enchantments apply to pickaxes and can be obtained via the enchanting table or anvil.
