## **Overview**

Falling toward a wall? **Right-click** it with a pickaxe. It bites into the surface and friction bleeds off your downward speed as you scrape along the edge. Harder blocks provide more resistance, slowing you down significantly faster. Once you've caught on, you don't have to stay put: hang on, slide, climb up, or kick off.

> **Note:** Higher-tier pickaxes hold onto surfaces longer and drain durability at a lower rate.

<br>

<div align="center">

| Block Type | Behavior | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hard Blocks**<br>*(Stone, Obsidian)* | Friction eats momentum every tick until you stop dead, hanging in place until grip or durability expires. Hold a pickaxe in **both hands** to stretch that hang time (x1.5 by default), then hold **Space** to climb. Reach a ledge you can stand on and you mantle up onto it. | **Full Stop / Lock-In / Climbable** |
| **Soft Blocks**<br>*(Dirt, Sand)* | No locking mechanic. You slide continuously down the face of the wall. | **Reduced Damage** |

</div>

<br>

> **Quick Escape:** Double-tap **Crouch** within **0.7 seconds** to kick yourself off the wall at any time, whether you are hanging still, climbing, or mid-slide.

## **Enchantments**

Apply these to pickaxes via an Enchanting Table or Anvil:

| Enchantment | Max Level | Effect |
| :--- | :-: | :--- |
| **Grip** | III | Extends how long you can hang onto a wall before slipping |
| **Momentum** | III | Reduces the forced cooldown length after slipping |
