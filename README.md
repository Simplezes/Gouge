## What it does

Falling toward a wall? Right-click it with a pickaxe. It bites into the surface and friction does the rest, bleeding off your speed as you scrape down. Harder blocks slow you down more.

**Hit stone or obsidian** and friction keeps eating your momentum every tick, no input needed, until you stop dead. Once you're stopped you hang there, locked in place, until your grip runs out or your pickaxe can't take any more punishment. When it gives out you slip, and there's a cooldown before you can grab again.

**Hit dirt or sand** and there's no locking, you just keep sliding, but your landing hits noticeably softer.

**Want off the wall early?** Double-tap crouch within 0.7 seconds. That kicks you off, whether you're hanging or still sliding, on hard blocks or soft ones.

Better pickaxes hang longer and drain slower.

## Enchantments

| Enchantment | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| **Grip** I to III | Hang onto the wall longer before you slip |
| **Momentum** I to III | Cuts the cooldown after you slip |

Enchant pickaxes with these at a table or anvil like anything else.
