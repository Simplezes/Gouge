## **Overview**

Falling toward a wall? **Right-click** it with a pickaxe. It bites into the surface and friction bleeds off your downward speed as you scrape along the edge. Harder blocks provide more resistance, slowing you down significantly faster.

> **Note:** Higher-tier pickaxes hold onto surfaces longer and drain durability at a lower rate.

<br>

<div align="center">

| Block Type | Behavior | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hard Blocks**<br>*(Stone, Obsidian)* | Friction eats momentum every tick until you stop dead, hanging in place until grip or durability expires. | **Full Stop / Lock-In** |
| **Soft Blocks**<br>*(Dirt, Sand)* | No locking mechanic. You slide continuously down the face of the wall. | **Reduced Damage** |

</div>

<br>

> **Quick Escape:** Double-tap **Crouch** within **0.7 seconds** to kick yourself off the wall at any time, whether you are hanging still or mid-slide.

## **Enchantments**

Apply these to pickaxes via an Enchanting Table or Anvil:

| Enchantment | Max Level | Effect |
| :--- | :-: | :--- |
| **Grip** | III | Extends how long you can hang onto a wall before slipping |
| **Momentum** | III | Reduces the forced cooldown length after slipping |
